Egg Float Challenge

Experiment Summary: Compare fresh water and salt water to see where an egg sinks or floats.

Difficulty
Easy
Easy to Hard

Supplies Needed

  • 2 eggs
  • Fresh water
  • Salt
  • 2 clear glasses

How To Do It

  1. Fill two glasses with water.
  2. Add lots of salt to one glass and stir well.
  3. Place an egg in each glass and compare what happens.

What To Notice

Watch what changes during the experiment, what causes the reaction or motion, and what you would change if you tried it again.

Safety note: Adult help is recommended whenever heat, glass, sharp items, or bigger reactions are involved. Do not eat or drink experiment materials unless the activity is clearly meant as a food project.
