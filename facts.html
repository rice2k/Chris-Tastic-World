Weird Science Facts

A fun retro facts page that feels like the original Chris-tastic World idea.

Featured Fact

Lightning is hotter than the surface of the sun, which is why it is such an amazing example of natural energy.

Why This Page Exists

Lightning Heat

Lightning can be hotter than the surface of the sun.

Banana Radiation

Bananas contain potassium, which is naturally a little radioactive.

Water Trick

Hot water can sometimes freeze faster than cold water under certain conditions.

Octopus Brains

An octopus has a central brain plus nerve clusters in its arms.

Space Smell

Astronauts have described space gear as smelling a bit like burnt metal after a spacewalk.

Glass Mystery

Glass looks solid, but it behaves in some ways like a very slow-moving liquid over huge time scales.

Brain Power

Your brain runs on tiny electrical signals all day long.

Honey Lasts

Honey can last for a very long time when sealed properly.

Static Fun

Static electricity can make small paper bits jump before you even touch them.

Color Split

Black marker ink can hide many different colors inside it.
