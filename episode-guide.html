Chris-tastic World Upgrade Plan

These are the next site upgrades to focus on so the experiment collection is easier to browse, safer to use, and more fun for kids.

2Add time, mess level, safety level, and science topic

Make it easy to print the supplies list, steps, or the full experiment page.
